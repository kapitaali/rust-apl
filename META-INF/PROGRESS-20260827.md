## Session 61 (2026-08-27, continued) — Complex numbers

### 1. Complex number literals — src/tokenizer.rs + parser.rs

Added the `reJim` complex literal syntax:

- `Tok::Complex(f64, f64)` token variant
- Tokenizer: `scan_number` now detects `J` after the real part and parses the imaginary part
- Examples: `1J2`, `¯3J¯4`, `0J1`
- Parser: `Tok::Complex(re, im)` → `Expr::Complex(re, im)`
- Eval: `Expr::Complex(re, im)` → `ValueP::scalar_from(Cell::complex(re, im))`

**Verified:** `1J2` → complex scalar, `1J2+2J3` → `3J5`, `1J2×2J3` → `¯4J7`. ✅

### 2. Circle function ○ complex support — src/functions.rs

Extended `A ○ B` for complex arguments:

- `9○B` — real part (works for real and complex)
- `11○B` — imaginary part (works for real and complex)
- `10○B` — magnitude |B|
- `12○B` — phase angle (arctan2(im, re))
- `¯12○B` — complex conjugate

**Verified:** `9○1J2` → 1, `11○1J2` → 2, `¯12○1J2` → `1J¯2`. ✅
Note: `10○1J2` (magnitude) matches within ⎕PP precision but not exactly — GNU APL rounds to ⎕PP=10.
